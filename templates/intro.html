01 — About

What is VisionGuard?

VisionGuard is an automated traffic enforcement system that uses AI to detect motorcycle riders who are not wearing helmets — and automatically deducts a fine of ₹1,000 from their registered bank account, without any human intervention.

Upload a video or image from a traffic camera. The system runs AI detection on every frame, identifies the violating vehicle's number plate using OCR, looks up the owner's account, and processes the fine instantly.

One-line pitch: "VisionGuard sees a rider without a helmet, reads their plate, and automatically deducts ₹1,000 from their account — no human needed."

03 — Pipeline

How It Works

From upload to fine — the full pipeline runs in a background thread while you watch live progress on screen.

01
Upload File Drop a video (MP4, AVI, MOV, MKV) or images (JPG, PNG) via the upload page. Multiple images can be processed in one batch.
02
YOLO Frame Detection YOLOv8 processes every 5th frame (FRAME_SKIP=5) detecting Bike, Rider, Helmet, No-Helmet, and License Plate classes at the set confidence threshold.
03
Violation Logic + OCR No-Helmet boxes overlapping Rider boxes trigger a violation. The plate region is cropped and passed to EasyOCR. Deduplication groups events within 2 seconds.
04
Fine Deduction trigger.py calls bank_engine.py with the plate number. If balance ≥ ₹1,000 the fine is deducted and a simulated SMS notification is logged to the transactions table.
05
Report & Output Annotated video and CSV report are saved. Results page shows compliance rate, violation count, and all owner details with download links.

05 — Technology

Technology Stack

🧠 YOLOv8 (Ultralytics)

State-of-the-art real-time object detection. Two custom-trained models: one for helmet/rider/bike classes, one for number plate localisation.

helmet_model.ptplate_model.pt
👁 EasyOCR

Reads alphanumeric text from the detected plate crop. Handles noisy or angled plates; output is cleaned with O/0 and I/1 position-aware correction.
